How to listen to Armstrong & Getty: live, on the radio, and on demand

An unofficial listener's guide · Last checked October 13, 2026 · Not affiliated with Armstrong & Getty or iHeartMedia · Something out of date? Email a correction

The Armstrong & Getty Show airs live weekdays from 6 to 10 a.m. Pacific (9 a.m. to 1 p.m. Eastern) on its flagship station, Talk 650 KSTE in Sacramento, and streams live on iHeartRadio. Stations elsewhere carry some or all of it, often at different times. Missed it? Every hour is posted to the official Armstrong & Getty On Demand podcast.

What time is Armstrong & Getty on?

On the flagship, Talk 650 KSTE, The Armstrong & Getty Show runs live on weekdays from 6:00 to 10:00 a.m. Pacific time, which is 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. Eastern. That's four hours. Other stations may carry fewer hours or air the show at a different time, so your local radio time can differ (see the station table).

Are Armstrong & Getty still on the radio? Are they live today?

Yes. As of October 13, 2026, Talk 650 KSTE lists The Armstrong & Getty Show weekdays from 6 to 10 a.m. Pacific, and that morning's hours were posted to the official On Demand podcast. On weekdays the show is live. On weekends the podcast carries a separate two-hour weekend edition, and on some holidays replay hours run instead of a new show.

Sources: KSTE schedule; Armstrong & Getty On Demand, checked October 13, 2026.

How do I stream Armstrong & Getty live for free?

The simplest free option is the flagship's live stream: open Talk 650 KSTE on iHeartRadio in a web browser, or search for "Talk 650 KSTE" in the iHeartRadio app. KSTE carries all four hours live. Many local stations also have a Listen Live button on their own websites, though they may not carry every hour.

  • Amazon Alexa: say "Alexa, play talk six fifty on iHeartRadio."
  • Google Assistant: say "Ok Google, play talk six fifty on iHeartRadio."
  • YouTube: we couldn't find an official YouTube live stream or full-episode uploads (checked October 2026).

Sources: iHeartRadio live page for Talk 650 KSTE; KSTE's smart-speaker instructions, checked October 13, 2026.

Why does my station play only part of the show?

The flagship airs all four hours, but each station decides how much of the show to carry and when. Of the stations above, KABC and KEX list three hours (6–9 a.m.), WMBD lists two (9–11 a.m. Central), and KPRC lists three overnight hours (2–5 a.m. Central). If your station's clock doesn't match 6–10 a.m. Pacific, it's carrying part of the show or airing it at a different time. To hear all four hours, stream KSTE live or catch the podcast afterward.

Missed it? How to listen later

Every hour of the weekday show is posted as its own episode of the official Armstrong & Getty On Demand podcast, usually within about half an hour after it airs. It's free on iHeartRadio, Apple Podcasts and Spotify, and in most other podcast apps. The show also has a short podcast-only extra called One More Thing.

Confused by the different feeds, or by hours that look out of order? Read the A&G podcasts, explained. Prefer each show day as one episode? Our free, unofficial app does that.

Listening in the car or on a speaker

  • Radio: tune to a station that carries the show (see the table or the official map).
  • Smart speaker, live: use the Alexa or Google phrases above.
  • Podcast: any podcast app you already use can play the On Demand feed.
  • This app (unofficial): download a whole show day for dead zones, cast it to Chromecast speakers from iPhone or Android, or use AirPlay on iPhone. CarPlay isn't supported.

Where this app fits, and where it doesn't

The Armstrong & Getty Podcast App doesn't play the live broadcast. It reads the official On Demand podcast feed and puts each show day's hours into one episode, in the order they were posted, including One More Thing when it's posted to that feed. It plays the same audio files as the feed, sponsor breaks included, and adds no ads of its own. It's a free, fan-built project, not affiliated with the show or iHeartMedia.
