The Armstrong & Getty Show.
Every hour. On demand.

Missed the show this morning? The app assembles every hour of every Armstrong & Getty show day into one clean daily episode. Download it for the commute, cast it to the kitchen speaker, and pick up right where you left off.

Why you'll like it

Built for one thing: never missing the show

No accounts, no ads in the app, no clutter — just The Armstrong & Getty Show, organized the way you actually listen to it.

One episode per show day

Every hour of the day's broadcast is stitched into a single daily episode, in order, with each segment a tap away.

Download & listen offline

Grab the whole show with one tap and listen anywhere — on the plane, in the canyon, wherever the signal dies.

Chromecast built in

Send the show to any Chromecast speaker or TV, from iPhone or Android, and keep control from your pocket.

Picks up where you stopped

Your spot in every episode is remembered — pause on the driveway, resume in the kitchen without hunting.

Real playback controls

Jump 10 or 30 seconds either way, change playback speed, or hop straight to the next segment of the show.

Fresh every morning

The app refreshes in the background so today's show is waiting for you — with lock-screen controls wherever you play it.

A quick look

Dark, focused, and fast

Real screenshots from the app — a gold-on-black theme that stays out of the way of the listening.

The show

Smart. Funny. Independent perspective.

Jack Armstrong and Joe Getty take an independent, contrarian look at news, politics, and modern life — merciless critics of mainstream media, on the air across the country every weekday morning. This app is a fan-built companion for listening to the show on your schedule; the show itself, its name, and all audio belong to Armstrong & Getty and iHeartMedia.
