The Armstrong & Getty podcasts, explained

An unofficial guide · Last checked October 13, 2026 · Links go to the official feeds · Not affiliated with Armstrong & Getty or iHeartMedia

Looking for the show itself? The official podcast is Armstrong & Getty On Demand, published by iHeartPodcasts, with every hour of the weekday show. It's free on iHeartRadio, Apple Podcasts and Spotify, and in most other podcast apps.

What is Armstrong & Getty On Demand?

Armstrong & Getty On Demand is the show's main official podcast, published by iHeartPodcasts. Each hour of the weekday radio show is posted as its own episode, usually within about half an hour after it airs, so a normal weekday's show is usually all there by late morning Pacific time. The feed also carries some extras, such as One More Thing and interviews. It's free to listen to.

What is Armstrong & Getty One More Thing?

One More Thing is a short, podcast-only companion to the show, posted most weekdays after the broadcast. It has its own feed, and its episodes are often posted to the On Demand feed as well. Listen on iHeartRadio or Apple Podcasts.

How a show day shows up in On Demand

The show airs live from 6 to 10 a.m. Pacific, and in our experience each hour usually shows up in On Demand within about half an hour of airing, so a normal weekday's show is usually all there by late morning Pacific time. When One More Thing is cross-posted, it tends to arrive after the broadcast ends. On weekends the feed carries a two-hour weekend edition, and on some holidays it posts replay hours, labeled as an A&G Replay, instead of a new show.

Why the hours can look out of order or missing

  • The titles aren't dates. Episode titles are usually a line from the show, not a date or hour number. When the hour number is given, it's in the episode description, and it isn't always written the same way.
  • Extras share the feed. One More Thing and some interviews are posted alongside the hours, so a weekday can bring more episodes than the show has hours.
  • The show isn't all there yet. Hours post through the morning, so before late morning Pacific time, today's later hours simply don't exist yet.
  • Holidays look different. Replay hours can appear instead of new ones.
  • Two feeds means duplicates. KSFO's station-run feed reposts hours separately, so following both it and On Demand gives you the same hour twice.

How to hear a whole show day in order

In any podcast app, subscribe to Armstrong & Getty On Demand, find that day's episodes and play them oldest first; if you're unsure, the episode description usually says which hour it is. Or use our app, which groups each show day into one episode in the order it was posted and plays the official audio files from the official feed.

Are there ads in the Armstrong & Getty podcast?

Yes. The official episodes include the show's sponsor breaks. Any app that plays the feed, including ours, plays them as published; our app adds no ads of its own.

More questions

Where can I listen to The Armstrong & Getty Show on demand?

In the official Armstrong & Getty On Demand podcast on iHeartRadio, Apple Podcasts, Spotify or most other podcast apps, all free. If you'd rather have each show day as one episode, our unofficial app does that.

What happened to Armstrong & Getty on Google Podcasts?

Google shut down its Podcasts app in the US in 2024 (TechCrunch), so the show is no longer available there. Use another podcast app; the On Demand feed works in almost all of them.

Are Select Cuts and Extra Large Interviews still updated?

As of October 13, 2026, Select Cuts last posted a new episode on February 27, 2026 and Extra Large Interviews on June 24, 2026. Interviews do still turn up in the On Demand feed.

Can I listen live instead?

Yes, on the radio or on iHeartRadio's Talk 650 KSTE stream. See how to listen to Armstrong & Getty live for times and stations.
